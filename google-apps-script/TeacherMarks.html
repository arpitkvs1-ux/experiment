How to use this page

  • Select Examination, then Subject.
  • If marks are already saved for that pair, you will see Edit marks and Download mark slip. Use them to change marks or get a PDF.
  • If nothing is saved yet, fill Maximum marks, Subject teacher, and Date of examination, enter each student’s marks (numbers or AB for absent), then tap Submit.
  • Use Enter or the up/down arrow keys to move between mark cells. Invalid values are cleared with a short message; fix the cell before moving on.
